# Artwork and fonts

The office is an imagined miniature, not a photograph of a physical company office. The selected day and evening images, duck cutout, and clean background plates were generated using the built-in image generation tool during the September 2026 design review. Their original prompts and iteration history remain on `backup/design-gallery-2026-09-15` in `docs/office-artwork.md` and `docs/office-play-artwork.md`.

The production site uses the approved assets unchanged. The day/evening switch is a crossfade between full images. The duck uses a 67 × 65 source-pixel clip from separate clean plates, with a cutout on top. It is not a replacement for the rest of the room.

The selected logo is direction 06, “Around the corner,” from the lettering review. It was isolated with the built-in image generation tool. The PNG has a white background; the header’s `mix-blend-mode: multiply` blends it into the page. The small “g” is the favicon. These are raster assets; no vector original is implied. Earlier logo directions remain on the backup branch or in the original local review outputs.

Source Sans 3 and Source Serif 4 are self-hosted under `public/fonts/`, with their license files alongside them. The shipped office does not use the discarded Unsplash composites or depend on third-party image/font requests.
